About Us

Built on passion, discipline and opportunity.

AkoStars Football Academy Limited is a registered football academy based in Utako, Abuja. We were founded with one goal: to give young people a real pathway in football, on and off the pitch.

Our training combines technical skill, tactical understanding and physical development with the values that make great players and great people: discipline, respect, teamwork and hard work.

Our Mission

To discover, develop and nurture young football talent through quality coaching, mentoring and opportunity.

Our Vision

To be a leading football academy in Nigeria, producing skilled, disciplined players who excel in football and in life.

Our Programmes

Training for every stage of the journey

From first touches to competitive football, each programme is designed around the age and ability of the player.

Ages 5 – 9

Grassroots

Fun, game-based sessions that build coordination, ball mastery and a love for the game.

  • Basic ball control & movement
  • Small-sided games
  • Confidence & teamwork
Ages 10 – 15

Youth Development

Structured training in technique, positioning and game understanding, with regular matches.

  • Passing, dribbling & finishing
  • Tactical awareness
  • Fitness & conditioning
Ages 16+

Elite Pathway

Advanced, competition-focused training for players aiming for the higher levels of the game.

  • High-intensity sessions
  • Competitive fixtures
  • Mentoring & career guidance
